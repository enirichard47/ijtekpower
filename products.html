What we supply

Whether you're building a new system, expanding one, or replacing worn-out components, we can help you source the right equipment. Availability, brands and specifications vary, so contact us for current options and pricing.

Solar Batteries

Energy storage keeps power available after sunset and during grid outages. We supply solar batteries for new systems, and for expanding or replacing the batteries in an existing set-up.

  • Battery storage for homes, schools, offices and businesses
  • Options for new systems and for upgrading or replacing existing batteries
  • Guidance on matching batteries to your inverter and backup needs

Solar Panels

Solar panels capture the energy that powers your system. We supply panels for new installations and for expanding systems already in place.

  • Panels for residential, commercial and community projects
  • Supply for new installations and system expansions
  • Advice on how many panels your energy needs call for
Enquire about solar panels

Inverters & Charge Controllers

Inverters and charge controllers convert and manage the power your panels produce and your batteries store. They sit at the heart of a dependable system.

  • Inverters for solar and backup power systems
  • Charge controllers to manage battery charging
  • Help choosing equipment that suits your loads and set-up
Enquire about inverters and charge controllers

Mounting Structures

Mounting structures hold panels securely in place. We supply frames, rails and fixings for roof-mounted and ground-mounted installations.

  • Frames and rails for roof and ground installations
  • Clamps, brackets and fixings
  • Options suited to different roof types

Cables & Protection Devices

The right cabling and protection devices help keep a solar system safe, organised and easier to maintain.

  • Breakers, isolators and surge protection
  • Distribution and combiner accessories
