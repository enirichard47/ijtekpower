Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services, products and free power audit.

Ask a question
Is the power audit really free?

Yes. The power audit and consultation are offered free of charge. They help you understand your energy needs before you decide on anything.

What happens during a power audit and consultation?

We talk through your property, your current set-up (if you have one), the appliances and equipment you want to power, and the problems you're experiencing. This helps us understand your requirements and recommend a suitable approach.

Do you work on older or existing systems?

Yes. Our diagnostics, repair and maintenance services cover both old and new solar and inverter systems.

Do you sell solar batteries and other solar materials?

Yes. We supply solar batteries and other solar materials, including panels, inverters, mounting structures, cables and protection devices. Availability and specifications vary, so please contact us for current options.

How much does a solar system cost?

It depends on what you need to power, how long you need backup for, and the equipment you choose. A free power audit is the best way to understand the right size of system for your property before you commit.

What types of properties do you work with?

We provide solar and inverter solutions for homes, schools, offices and businesses, and communities.

Where are you located?

Our office is at No. 22, Alhaji Yusuf Street, Igbogbo, Ikorodu, Lagos, Nigeria. Contact us to confirm whether we can serve your location.
